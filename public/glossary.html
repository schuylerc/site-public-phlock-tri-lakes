GLOSSARY

Speaking Parrot Head

Parrot Heads ~ We are Jimmy Buffett fans! We’re Trop Rock music listening, flip-flop, flowered shirt and lei wearing, lovin & livin the island (Lake) life in a Margaritaville state of mind phun folks!
Parakeets ~ Offspring of a Parrot Head.
P.H.I.P. ~ Parrot Heads in Paradise. The “Mother Bird” PHIP.com
Fins Up ~ The international sign for Parrot Heads.
P.W.A.P. ~ Party With A Purpose. Our Phlock’s P.W.A.P. is a charity event benefiting The Golden Corner Food Pantry. The event includes music featuring nationally know singer/song writers, silent auctions/raffles, food, shenanigans & phun, always phun!
Trop Rock ~ Tropical rock (also known as Gulf & Western) is a genre of popular music that incorporates elements and influences of rock, reggae, country, folk, calypso, zydeco, and pop music, with themes and musical compositions inspired by an island style.
W.T.B. ~ Woman to Blame
M.O.T.M. ~ Meeting of the Minds ~ The annual convention of Parrot Heads in Paradise will be held at Gulf Shores, Alabama. Annually, PHIP holds MOTM that is open to all dues paying club members on a first come/first served basis. Read more at MOTM.rocks
Margaritaville ~ Where Parrot Heads live if only in their minds. Margaritaville is where Jimmy lost his shaker of salt, stepped on a pop top, boiled shrimp, gotta tattoo and figured out it’s his own damn fault. Restaurants, gift shops, hotels, cruise line, RV parks, adult communities...oh my!
C.R.B. ~ Coral Reefer Band/Jimmy Buffett’s band

Phlock of the Tri-Lakes

A Parrot Head Club serving Lake Keowee, Lake Hartwell, and Lake Jocassee in beautiful Upstate South Carolina.
