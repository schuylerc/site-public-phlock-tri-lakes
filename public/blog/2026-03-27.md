# March Board Recap

**March 27, 2026**

Well Phlock, it is already the end of March!!  Crazy how time flies.  Your board has been very very busy.  

Some of the highlights are:

- Due to some legal issues, we had to dissolve our club, Phlock of the Tri Lakes Parrothead Club. We refiled for a new entity and we are now **The Phlock of the Tri Lakes**. We are a 501c(7). This has been very tedious and time-consuming. Special thanks to Sharon and Mike Manning, and Joe Brydon for all their work and guidance. 
- We are in the process of upgrading our website and rehosting on a different service. This will save us money as well as provide a platform that we can leverage into the future.
- The biggest undertaking we have taken on is our moving to the **PYLI application** to provide an integrated platform for our club to communicate calendar and event information, conduct surveys of our members, and provide a single financial portal for ticket sales and dues payment. We are still in the rollout stages and encourage you to download the app.  
- **Party with a Purpose 2026** will be July 25th at Drake Landing. The artists will be Lattitude (a husband and wife duo). We will need many volunteers to step up for all the different tasks that need to be addressed.
- We have 12 couples signed up for **Meeting of the Mayans** in January 2027. Looking forward to great times with this group!
- On a final note, **Erica Sunshine Lee** will be performing for us on June 5th. More on that to come.

Hopefully, this update will give you a sense of how committed your board and committee members are to the continuing Phun we are working on for all of you!

All the Best,
